# Nature Cams — Ohio & the Virginia Mountains

A free, static page of public nature, trail, and wildlife cams from Ohio and the Virginia mountains (Shenandoah, the Blue Ridge, and Appalachian Virginia).

Live site: https://darkgamegod.github.io/nature-cams/

No accounts, API keys, or paid services. The page is plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Cams included

Checked the evening of Sunday, November 22, 2026 (America/New_York).

### Ohio — live video

- Northeast Ohio Bird Feeder Cam (Bird Watching HQ, Akron area near Cuyahoga Valley National Park) — YouTube
- Northeast Ohio Backyard Wildlife Cam (Bird Watching HQ, ground-level feeder) — YouTube
- Brian's Birds Unlimited Feeder Cam (Strongsville) — YouTube
- Little Miami Bald Eagle Cam 1 and Cam 2 (Little Miami Conservancy, Little Miami River) — YouTube
- Little Miami Osprey Cam (Little Miami Conservancy) — YouTube
- Bonnie & Clyde Eagle Cam (Cardinal Land Conservancy, Bortz Family Nature Preserve, Cincinnati) — opens the official site; the player cannot be embedded here
- Lake Metroparks Wildlife Center Cam (bobcat in care at Penitentiary Glen Reservation) — official / click2stream player

### Virginia mountains — live video

- Big Meadows Live (Shenandoah National Park, Skyline Drive mile 51) — opens the National Park Service page
- Shenandoah Valley Webcam (view of Marys Rock, Stony Man, and Hawksbill) — opens the NPS page
- Virginia Elk Cam (Virginia Department of Wildlife Resources, Buchanan County) — opens the official DWR page
- Wildlife Center of Virginia Critter Cams (Waynesboro; Buddy the bald eagle and black bear cubs) — opens the official page

### Virginia mountains — snapshot

- Mountain View Cam (Pinnacles, Skyline Drive mile 36.7, looking toward Luray) — still image from the NPS / PhenoCam feed, updated on its own schedule

## Notes

Some cams go offline at night, in winter, or when solar batteries run low. YouTube video IDs can change when an operator restarts a stream. National Park Service, Virginia DWR, and Cardinal Land Conservancy players are hosted on HDOnTap and only allow embeds on their own sites, so those cards link out.

Left out after checking on November 22, 2026: the Avon Lake Eagle Cam was not actually live; Blue Ridge wildlife cams from The Robertson Rottie Ranch were offline (their channel was running a fireplace ambience stream instead); the official Big Meadows still-image file on nps.gov was blocked from this environment (the live Big Meadows cam is included instead).
